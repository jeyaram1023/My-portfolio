02 · Threat Model

Wireless exposure is a structural flaw.

Nearly every proposed improvement in access-control secures a wireless channel rather than removing it. The attack surface is the radio link itself.

A relay attack doesn't need to break advanced encryption. It only needs two radios bridging the physical distance between a keyfob on a kitchen counter and a vehicle parked outside.

WireLock's premise is a structural paradigm shift: if the Key Microcontroller physically cannot transmit over the air, there is no signal left to relay, replay, amplify, or clone.

RELAY ATTACKS [MITIGATED]
Zero antennas mean signal amplifiers cannot bridge the physical distance to the key token.
REPLAY ATTACKS [MITIGATED]
Captured communications are instantly invalidated by a strict, time-sensitive physical handshake.
RFID CLONING [MITIGATED]
Static IDs are cryptographically obscured and dynamically mutated prior to physical transmission.
